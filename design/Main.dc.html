The Stack

The sidebar becomes the wordmark. Every workspace is a card in a pile — yours at the top, always — and the one you are in is pulled out and opened, its navigation living inside it. The others stay visible as coloured spines you can reach at any time.

The structural change: there is no Spaces row any more, and no switcher dropdown. The stack is the list of workspaces, so switching is one click on something already on screen instead of two clicks into a menu. Which workspace you are in is a shape, not a label.

The pile survives the collapse: still a stack, still one click to switch.

One click, not three
Every workspace is on screen at all times. Switching never opens a menu, and there is no Spaces page to visit first.
Where you are is a shape
The open card is the only card with white ground and a lifted shadow. You can tell at a glance, from across the room, without reading a word.
It is the logo
Five stacked bars, on every screen, doing a job. The brand stops being a mark in a corner and becomes the way the app is organised.
